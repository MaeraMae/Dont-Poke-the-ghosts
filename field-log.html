The Host's Private Notebook

Field Log

Pages from the notebook kept between calls — the observations that didn't fit in the episode, written for no one in particular.

Alabama · The White Thang

On faceless things

Billy Ray's dogs had it right before he did. That's not a coincidence I take lightly — the animals know first, always, and their instinct predates ours by a long, long time.

White entities in folklore almost universally represent boundary-crossers — things that exist between states of being. This one has no face because it doesn't need one where it comes from.

— salt the thresholds, and don't let anyone scoff at you for it.

Alaska · The Iliamna Lake Monster

What the water keeps

A lake the size of Connecticut that science hasn't bothered to study properly. Pete flies over it and calls what he sees a shape, not a name, which is the correct amount of caution.

Water is the oldest mirror — what lives beneath it reflects something true about a place.

— aerial observation from a distance is how you gather data without disturbing whatever has claimed that territory.

Kentucky · The Kelly-Hopkinsville Goblins

On eleven witnesses

Eleven people, multiple hours, documented by police, an account unchanged after nearly seventy years. I apply rigorous standards to these things. This one holds.

Not everything that walks wrong is broken. Some things simply weren't built to our specifications.

— floating rather than walking is not a malfunction. It's a different set of rules entirely.

Louisiana · The Rougarou

Old magic, French roots, Louisiana soil

Tante Marie-Claire didn't need convincing. She called to inform, not to ask. I extend professional respect from one practitioner to another whenever I get the chance.

The Atchafalaya Basin at night is not a place for the poorly prepared or the spiritually ungrounded.

— go in with protection, with purpose, and with someone who already knows the water.

New Jersey · The Jersey Devil

Three hundred years and counting

Frank waited a long time for this call to happen. The oldest named cryptid in North America deserves the full weight of that title, and I told him so.

The Pine Barrens is a million acres of genuine wilderness sitting in the most densely populated state in America. Of course something lives there.

— a thousand witnesses over multiple days in 1909 is not mass hysteria. It's a mass event.

Ohio · The Loveland Frogmen

Fifty years, filed and fine

Officer Dave locked his doors, filed a report, and came back half a century later to talk about it on a podcast. That is a complete and healthy relationship with an unexplained encounter.

A figure holding a wand that sprayed sparks is a detail I return to regularly. I've studied magical tools across dozens of traditions.

— write it down the day it happens. Dave's version only got clearer with distance.

South Carolina · The Lizard Man of Scape Ore Swamp

Documented damage

Christopher was seventeen. The car had scratches the police photographed. Thirty-five years later he called sounding entirely at peace, which is its own kind of evidence.

Bipedal, scaled, green, with red eyes — I've catalogued this morphology across the Southeast with enough consistency to suggest a real category.

— thirty-five years of quiet acceptance is what I'd call spiritual maturity, usually reached by other means.

Vermont · Emily's Bridge

A bridge with opinions

Cal fixes old bridges for a living. He did not expect this one to have feelings about being fixed. His crew keeps leaving early, and I don't think that's a scheduling problem.

Scratch marks on cars parked overnight, photographed by multiple independent visitors. I always prioritize repeatable physical evidence.

— a brief, sincere acknowledgment at the bridge itself, before the next crew arrives. It costs nothing.

Washington · Sasquatch

The professor from Idaho State

I asked more questions than I offered opinions this time, on purpose. Some guests know more about the subject than I ever will, and Dr. Meldrum has spent a career on this.

Dermal ridges in certain footprint casts are, in my assessment, the most scientifically robust physical evidence in the cryptid literature. They cannot be faked with a costume.

— sixty years of debunking attempts, and the film is still standing. Note that.

West Virginia · The Mothman

Donna calls every November 15th

I know her number by heart at this point. That is not obsession. That is witness, kept faithfully, year after year, whether or not anyone else is still listening.

John Keel documented over one hundred witnesses during the active period. That is methodical journalism that has never been adequately credited as such.

— some hauntings aren't a cause. They're a warning that arrived on time and wasn't heeded.

Wyoming · Devil's Tower & the Library

Closing the map

Fifty states, and I saved Wyoming's mummy for last on purpose. Some records deserve to be the final word, not because the season is over, but because the search for Pedro isn't.

Finding Pedro's current location is the most important open task in American cryptid research. Someone, somewhere, has him or knows where he is.

— the map is finished. The work, as always, is not.
