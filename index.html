The Format

One call. One state. One thing that shouldn't be there.

Don't Poke the Ghosts runs on real 911-style urgency about entirely unreal problems. A rancher whose cattle won't drink from the pond. A ranger who found tracks going in a circle they can't explain. A retired cop who's been sitting on a story for fifty years. They call. The host listens, then delivers the legend, the history, the sources, and — always — practical advice, delivered with equal parts reverence and dry humor.

🕯️ History & Context

Every entity gets the real background — first sightings, primary sources, and the cultural weight behind the legend.

📖 Field-Tested Advice

Salt lines, sonar, spreadsheets — the host takes every caller's situation seriously and tells them what to actually do next.

🗺️ All 50 States

One state per month, cryptid segment and urban legend segment both, until the whole map is covered.
